How I Balance Being a Student, Developer & Graphic Designer

People always ask me: "How do you do it all?"

The truth? I don't. Not perfectly, anyway.

I'm a student. I'm a developer. I'm also a graphic designer. Three different hats, one brain, and a very messy schedule.

Some days I'm drowning in lecture notes. Other days I'm deep in code, trying to fix a bug that makes no sense. And somewhere in between, I'm tweaking a poster design or a brand identity for a client.

It's chaotic. But I've figured out a few things that help me stay afloat.

My Daily Reality

Let me paint you a picture of a typical day:

  • Morning: Classes. Trying to stay awake during 8 AM lectures while fighting the urge to check my phone.
  • Afternoon: Rushed lunch, then straight into freelance design work or coding projects. This is usually my most productive window.
  • Evening: More classes or group work. The struggle of coordinating with teammates who have different schedules.
  • Night: My real work happens here. 10 PM to 2 AM is when I'm actually building stuff. Yes, my sleep schedule is a mess.

This isn't a flex. It's exhausting. But it's also the reality of trying to build a career while still being a student.

What Actually Works

After trial and error, here are the strategies that actually help:

  • I stopped trying to do everything daily. Some days are for code. Some are for design. Some are just for being a student. I rotate focus instead of juggling everything at once.
  • I leverage my overlap. When I'm designing, I think about UI/UX. When I'm coding, I design systems. The two skills feed each other.
  • I say no. Not to everything, but to things that don't serve my goals. That was hard to learn.
  • I accept that something will always suffer. Some weeks, my grades slip. Some weeks, I don't code at all. And that's okay.

The Hard Truth

I'm not a productivity guru. I don't have a perfect system. I don't wake up at 5 AM and crush a "morning routine."

But I show up. Every day. Even when I'm tired. Even when I'd rather watch Netflix. Even when I feel like I'm failing at everything at once.

To anyone out there trying to balance multiple things: you're not alone. It's hard. It's messy. But it's worth it.

Just remember to sleep sometimes. I'm still working on that part.

Thanks for reading!
