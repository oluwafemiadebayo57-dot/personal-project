Confession: 90% of My Side Projects Are Abandoned

Everyone says "build projects!" but no one talks about the graveyard of unfinished repos on our GitHub profiles.

So let me be honest: I've started dozens of side projects. Most of them are dead. Abandoned. Archived forever.

And honestly? That's fine.

The Reality

Side projects usually go like this:

  • Big idea phase: "This will change everything!"
  • Hype phase: Coding non-stop. Everything works. Feel invincible.
  • Reality phase: The interesting problems are solved. Now it's just boring work. CSS. Edge cases. Docs.
  • Distraction phase: Life happens. Work gets busy. New shiny thing appears.
  • Ghost phase: You realize you haven't touched it in weeks. You try to go back but... the magic is gone.

Why We Stop

  • We chase the learning, not the product
  • Once we figure out how to build it, the motivation dies
  • Our day jobs already take our best coding energy
  • Maintenance is boring (and who does that for free?)

The Truth

Those abandoned repos weren't failures. They were experiments.

Every dead project taught me something:

  • That dead API wrapper? Learned HTTP clients.
  • That half-built dashboard? Learned data viz.
  • That abandoned mobile app? Learned I hate mobile dev.

90% failure is the cost of becoming a better developer.

What I've Learned

  • Archive, don't delete. Read-only repos = no guilt.
  • You don't need to ship to learn. Build 20%, get 80% of the knowledge.
  • It's normal. Every developer has a graveyard. We just don't show it.

Your GitHub graveyard isn't embarrassing. It's proof you keep trying.

One of these attempts will stick. And when it does, you'll be glad you built all the "failures" that came before it.
