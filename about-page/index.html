Make it

I sketch wireframes and make prototypes. Talking through tactile designs existing in the browser is worth its weight. Design tools only carry you so far; the rest should be realized with a link my team can rally around.

02

Collaborate

Good design is not created in a vacuum but rather in a shared space. It must be facilitated and iterated upon as a team. I aim to include stakeholders in my design process and create a collaborative environment that welcomes and encourages feedback.

03

Build Performant Code

Performance is not a feature; it's a requirement. I focus on optimizing asset delivery, minimizing render-blocking resources, and writing efficient JavaScript to ensure fast loading times and a smooth 60fps interaction experience for every user.

04

Maintain Quality & Scale

I prioritize code health through strict component architecture, proper typing (e.g., TypeScript), and comprehensive unit testing. This approach ensures the codebase remains maintainable, scalable, and resilient as the product grows.

Before I wrote my first line of production code, I spent my creative energy in the world of graphic design. Transforming ideas into visual identities and digital assets gave me a deep appreciation for user experience, layout harmony, and aesthetic precision. Over time, I found myself wanting to take those static designs a step further—not just making them look compelling on a screen, but actually bringing them to life and making them interactive.

As my curiosity grew, I transitioned into web development, diving headfirst into building functional, dynamic projects of my own. From crafting responsive interfaces to experimenting with complex layouts and systems, every project has pushed me to bridge the gap between design and engineering. Today, I don't just visualize how a product should look; I build how it functions behind the scenes.

Balancing both graphic design and web development allows me to approach technical challenges with a unique dual perspective. I bring an eye for detail, color theory, and visual hierarchy to my code, ensuring that the applications I build are as polished and user-friendly as they are robust. Outside of building digital experiences, I enjoy working on personal creative projects, cooking, learning new skills, and continuously improving the systems around me. I'm driven by a genuine love for building, solving problems, and growing one step at a time.
